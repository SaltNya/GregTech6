# Issue 14: collected rock replacement

Sneak-use a pebble or raw ore on a sturdy top surface to replace one collected item. Both loaders preserve its exact registered item and count. It does not roll a second ore bonus. The native placement boundary checks building permission, bounds, support and entity obstruction. Non-sneak use passes through.

Both native-world checks passed with a GT granite pebble and vanilla raw copper. Source hashes and license evidence are in [the record](rock-replacement-20261005.json).
